# Arizona Coyotes Statistical Database (1996–2024)

A tribute site and workbook for the Arizona identity of the franchise (Phoenix 1996–2014, Arizona 2014–2024).
After the NHL's Sept. 24, 2026 consolidation, the original Winnipeg Jets history belongs to the Jets, so this project covers Arizona only.

## Files
- `arizona_coyotes_definitive_stat_database.xlsx` — Seasons (with GF/GA/GD and playoff splits), Skaters, Goalies, Draft (all 226 picks), Playoff Series, Playoffs Skaters, Playoffs Goalies, Leadership, Facts, Honors, All-Stars, Records, Timeline, Sources (1,000+ live formulas: GP, Pts, P/GP, GD, Win%, totals)
- `ArizonaCoyotesTribute.jsx` — React component (default export), same data embedded
- `arizona-coyotes-tribute.html` — standalone version of the site

## What's inside
All 28 seasons (with leading scorer, team MVP, hardest-working player and Man of the Year), every draft pick, playoff history and playoff stats for every postseason player, captains/coaches/GMs/owners/arenas, a points-by-season chart, facts and firsts, roster breakdowns by nation/position/decade, coaching eras, legends, awards/Hall of Fame/Ring of Honor, every Coyotes All-Star, the record book, the 2012 run, a timeline, and a searchable, sortable archive of all 438 players.

## Scope notes
- Complete roster: 388 skaters and 50 goalies, every player who appeared in at least one game for Arizona (Wikipedia's all-time list). A few late-2023–24 call-ups may be missing.
- Career stats are Arizona-only. Doan's Arizona line (1,466 GP, 395 G, 560 A, 955 pts) comes from Elite Prospects; Wikipedia's row includes his 1995–96 Winnipeg games.
- Season records: W-L-T through 1998–99, W-L-T-OTL 1999–2004, W-L-OTL from 2005–06.

## Sources
Wikipedia (seasons, players, award winners), Elite Prospects, NHL.com, Gametime franchise-records summary.
